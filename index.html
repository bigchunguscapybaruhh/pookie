🎃 Mowzkitow: Halloween Town 🐈‍⬛

Last time (English): Mowzkitow fought through the forest maze, recovered all 10 braincells (+boost strength!), made one villainous enemy — the judging kitty — and befriended the local kitties.

Now: she wanders into a spooky open-world 2000s Japanese town at night — streets, dark back alleys, shops, strays, raccoons, cops, dogs running a ramen joint... and rumors of a cat gang recruiting at the rundown NEKO PUB.

  • 🔎 First quest: speak to the veteran kitty at the pub (west side, red lanterns)
  • 💻 Side hustle: NET CAFE 24H north side — typing races vs NPCs from 40 to 100 WPM. Winners get 3Ⓜ and town fame
  • 🕳 Witch cave in the NE corner — cryptic witch cats, a brew that turns you into a cat for 30s, zero explanations
  • 🍖 Hunger drains from 100 — eat ramen (5Ⓜ), soda (2Ⓜ), onigiri (3Ⓜ) or starve
  • 🐾 Earn cat-gang standing, dodge the cops, survive the alleys
  • ⚔️ At 50 standing... something in the east alley stirs
